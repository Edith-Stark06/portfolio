Alzheimer's Disease Classification using ResNet-DeiT Hybrid Model

IEEE ICDSBS 2026 Accepted & Published
arrow_forward

This paper presents a novel hybrid architecture combining Deep Residual Networks (ResNet) with Data-efficient Image Transformers (DeiT) for the early classification and staging of Alzheimer's Disease from MRI scans. By leveraging the local feature extraction capabilities of CNNs with the global contextual awareness of Vision Transformers, the model achieves state-of-the-art accuracy on the ADNI dataset while maintaining computational efficiency suitable for clinical deployment.

Gradient Boosting Ensemble for Diabetic Complication Prediction

IC3DCM 2026 Accepted & Published
arrow_forward

We developed an advanced predictive model utilizing a heterogeneous ensemble of Gradient Boosting algorithms (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost) to forecast the onset of severe diabetic complications using longitudinal electronic health records. The study demonstrates significant improvements in precision-recall AUC over baseline clinical scoring systems, incorporating temporal feature engineering to capture disease progression dynamics.

Sepsis Treatment Optimization using Reinforcement Learning

MIMIC-III Dataset Under Peer Review
arrow_forward

This research formulates sepsis management in the ICU as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) and employs Deep Q-Networks (DQN) to derive optimal dynamic treatment regimens for intravenous fluid and vasopressor administration. Validated retrospectively on the MIMIC-III database, the RL agent's policies demonstrate potential to reduce patient mortality by aligning more closely with expert clinician actions while avoiding over-resuscitation.
